Two systems I have built and that are in daily use. Both started with the same question: what is taking the team too much time?

Logistics Live

DSPOps

Delivery companies that work for Amazon run dozens of drivers and vans every day. The rota, vehicle checks, compliance dates and the daily deployment plan tend to live in separate spreadsheets and chat groups, and the morning depends on someone holding it all in their head.

DSPOps brings that into one place: who is driving, which vans are ready, what is due for renewal and what needs attention before the shift starts. It includes an AI check that flags damage in van photos.

Visit dspops.app
Property Live

Estate Revenue Manager

Firms that staff high-end residential buildings need to know who is covering each site, what that cover earns and where a shift was missed. Keeping the schedule and the revenue in separate places makes the monthly reporting a job in itself.

This system holds the staff schedule and the revenue together, so cover, confirmed and missed income and the forecast for the months ahead come out of one tool, with reports ready to export.

Common questions

What does it cost?

Every project has different requirements, so there is no standard price. The first call is free. After that there is a consultation fee for the planning session, agreed before we book it. You come out of that session with a written plan and one fixed price for building it, yours to keep either way. If you go ahead, a small deposit starts the work and the rest is paid on milestones. No hourly billing, no open-ended retainers.

Do we need to replace our existing tools?

No. I build around what you have: your spreadsheets, email, calendars and the software you already pay for. The new system connects them and fills the gaps, so your team isn't forced to start from scratch.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the system, so the timeline is set out in the written plan before any building starts. Smaller pieces can go live early, and on a larger system you see working parts as they land rather than waiting for one big reveal at the end.

What support is included after launch?

An agreed support period is included in the fixed price, so it is not sold back to you afterwards. What it covers and how long it runs is agreed in the plan before work starts.

What if I don't know what I need?

That is what the free call is for. You tell me how your team works and where the time goes, and I'll point out what could be simpler, even if you don't know where to start. If there is nothing worth building, I'll tell you that too.
